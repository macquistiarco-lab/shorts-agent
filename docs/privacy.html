Privacy Policy - Shorts Publisher

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Shorts Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to manage the comments on those videos.

Data accessed

With the user's consent, the app requests two Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, to the authorized YouTube channel;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl, used to read the channel's own video list and schedule, to read viewers' comments on the channel's own videos, and to post the channel's first comment and its replies.

It does not delete content and it does not access any other Google data.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own devices and in the owner's private cloud environment that runs the tool. No personal data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties, and no analytics or tracking are used.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
